by That Guy (Coronerra)

Play as a werewolf in OStim with your mind intact.

ONE DOWNLOAD FOR EVERYONE (NEW IN 0.7)
--------------------------------------
0.7 replaces every earlier OStim Beast Form download (Merged, the TWO
Edition, the Merged TWO Patch and the TWO Herm Patch). There is one zip with
one installer, and every setup uses the TWO - Total Werewolf Overhaul 4.1
body by Frosferes. TWO is REQUIRED and NOT included:
  https://www.loverslab.com/topic/159911-two-total-werewolf-overhaul/

This mod uses the TWO body made by Frosferes, at the LoversLab community's
request. Frosferes is okay with patches but I will not repackage any files
he made that I did not have to patch. So non-softbody female bodies and all
Male bodies are still dependant on his TWO bodies. Anyone is free to make
their own patches, hubs and mods based on this one.

THE INSTALLER (read every page; wrong options = the mod won't work)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. TWO user or Growl user (pick one)
   - Growl user (ticked by default, recommended for Nolvus users):
     OStim_Beast_Form_Merged.esp + OStim_Beast_Form_Merged_TWO_Patch.esp +
     OStim_Beast_Form_Merged_TWO_Patch - BFA Heads.esp. Same plugins as
     Merged 0.6 + the Merged TWO Patch 0.1.
     Growl: https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/31245
   - TWO user (no Growl):
     OStim_Beast_Form_TWO.esp + OStim_Beast_Form_TWO - BFA Heads.esp. Same
     plugin as OStim Beast Form - TWO Edition 0.2.
2. Do you use softbody? (pick one)
   - No (ticked by default): nothing softbody-related is installed. In
     scenes, male werewolves get TWO's own hard canine (TWO Core file).
     UNP users, and CBBE / UBE users without softbody, pick No.
   - Yes (since 0.7.2): male werewolves wear TWO's hard canine in scenes,
     with a collision setup GT Softbody bodies react to. Female werewolves
     get a softbody-ready copy of TWO's female body (stretchable vaginal
     canal with its own collision). Both are edited copies of TWO's meshes
     in this mod's own folder; TWO's files are not overwritten. No GT
     Softbody files are shipped.
     Yes also installs Scripts\OCumMaleScript.pex: the OCum + Tull/Liquid
     + GT Softbody merged script (same as OCum-Tull-Liquid-Softbody-Patch
     v1.0). It falls back cleanly without Tull / Liquid Pack. It must win
     over GT Softbody's optional OCum patch: load this mod after GT -
     Softbody in MO2's left pane.
     SOFTBODY: https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/152103
3. Hermaphrodite patch? A.K.A. Fruta? (optional, unticked)
   For TWO Extras' herm / urta PLAYER werewolf. Installs
   OStim_Beast_Form_TWO_Herm_Patch.esp; works with either page-1 choice.
   Needs TWO Extras (WerewolvesExtras.esp), plus WerewolvesExtrasGrowl.esp
   if you use Growl. Leave OStim's "Futas use male role" on.
   I am not going to test this myself. If you use TWO's herm or urta
   werewolf, please test it and report any problems in the support section
   of the OStim Beast Form Merged page on LoversLab.
4. Read and acknowledge: one required box, "I have read and understand".

REQUIREMENTS
------------
Everyone: TWO 4.1 (Core), Monster Facial Animation (MonsterLipSync1.esp, a
hard master), OStim Standalone, OCum Ascended, SKSE, Address Library, SkyUI,
PapyrusUtil, RaceMenu, powerofthree's Papyrus Extender, XPMSSE, FSMP,
Pandora (or another behavior engine), and at least one OStim pack with
werewolf scenes (for example Lirael Atelier's free converted packs on
LoversLab, account Lauffey).
Growl option: Growl, JS Emissive Eyes SE, TWO's Growl patch
(WerewolvesGrowl.esp).
Softbody Yes: SOFTBODY (GT - Softbody) by Goutou. This mod must load after it
(left pane) so its OCumMaleScript.pex wins.
NOT needed: HDT Werewolves (it must be OFF; its plugin is also called
Werewolves.esp), ABC, Creature Framework, HCOS, SOS, SexLab.
Optional: Better Facial Animations (tick WerewolfBeastRace and
DLC1VampireBeastRace in its race filters), OCreatures Reforged, SkyrimNet +
OStimNet + OStim Navigator, Tull / Liquid creampie meshes, TWO Alternate
Textures, Ostrands with Werewolves (separate download, below).

KNOWN INCOMPATIBILITIES (AS OF THIS RELEASE)
--------------------------------------------
- Diverse Werewolves Collection SE by opusGlass: with it installed, the
  camera locks to the sides. Leave it off with this mod.
  https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/7009
- TWO's TWO_SkinSwapper.dll (v0.2.1, part of TWO Extras) by Frosferes: it
  does not load on Skyrim SE 1.7.99 / 1.7.104 or newer, because it doesn't
  support the new Address Library format. TWO's gender / herm skin spells
  then have no effect. This is a limit of TWO's DLL and the game version,
  not a bug in this mod. This mod's Hermaphrodite option doesn't use that
  DLL, but TWO's herm / urta body is swapped in by it, so on those game
  versions use 1.6.1170 (or GOG 1.6.1179) for the herm body until TWO's DLL
  is updated.
  https://www.loverslab.com/topic/159911-two-total-werewolf-overhaul/

INSTALL (MO2)
-------------
1. Install the requirements and TWO 4.1. Disable HDT Werewolves.
2. Coming from an earlier OStim Beast Form download? Read UPDATING first.
3. Install this zip and answer the four pages.
4. Left pane: this mod below OStim, OCum, Growl, GT - Softbody,
   OCreatures Reforged, the animation packs and SkyrimNet/OStimNet. Put TWO
   (then TWO Alternate Textures, then your TWO BodySlide output) below this
   mod. Better Facial Animations and the Pandora output go last. MFA shares
   no files with this mod (since 0.7.1), so its spot doesn't matter.
5. Right pane, by hand (no LOOT):
   Growl option:
     MonsterLipSync1.esp, Growl - Werebeasts of Skyrim.esp (+ patches),
     OCreatures.esp (optional), Werewolves.esp, WerewolvesGrowl.esp,
     OStim_Beast_Form_Merged.esp,
     OStim_Beast_Form_Merged_TWO_Patch.esp            (directly below it)
     OStim_Beast_Form_Merged_TWO_Patch - BFA Heads.esp
     OStim_Beast_Form_TWO_Herm_Patch.esp              (Herm option only)
   TWO option:
     MonsterLipSync1.esp, OCreatures.esp (optional), Werewolves.esp (+ other
     Werewolves*.esp), OStim_Beast_Form_TWO.esp,
     OStim_Beast_Form_TWO - BFA Heads.esp,
     OStim_Beast_Form_TWO_Herm_Patch.esp              (Herm option only)
   NOLVUS: never place a plugin below Paper Maps. Put these as low as you can
   but ABOVE the Paper Maps plugins.
6. Run Pandora.
7. In game, open the "OStim Beast Form" MCM and set the start-scene hotkey.

HERMAPHRODITE OPTION AND SKYRIM 1.7.99+
---------------------------------------
TWO's herm / urta body is swapped in by TWO's own TWO_SkinSwapper.dll (TWO
Extras, 0.2.1 from 2022). It supports Skyrim 1.5.97 and 1.6.x, not the new
Address Library format of 1.7.99 / 1.7.104, so SKSE reports an error for it
and the gender spell changes nothing visible. That's TWO's DLL, not this mod.
Use 1.6.1170 (or GOG 1.6.1179) for the herm body until TWO's DLL is updated.
Even then, the spell takes effect on your next transformation.
